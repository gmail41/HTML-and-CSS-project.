Strategy

How we invest

Active renovation and resale paired with scaled rental income.

Strategy 1

Renovate & Sell

This is the active, value-add side of the business. We identify properties where renovation can meaningfully improve condition and value, then bring them back to market.

  1. AcquireSource and underwrite properties against strict return criteria.
  2. RenovateExecute a defined scope of work with vetted contractors on a set schedule and budget.
  3. SellList and sell to buyers, returning capital to fund the next acquisition.
Strategy 2

Buy & Hold Rentals

This is the income side of the business. We keep selected properties as long-term rentals, generating recurring cash flow with professional, hands-on management.

  • Recurring rental income from a diversified set of properties
  • Consistent maintenance and tenant management standards
  • Long-term appreciation potential on held assets
  • Passive income profile that complements renovation profits
Operating model

How the two strategies fit together

Our model draws on private real estate equity (value-add investing) and on income-focused, REIT-style ownership of a large rental base. The table compares the two sides of the platform.

ObjectiveCapital gains through value creationRecurring income and long-term appreciation
