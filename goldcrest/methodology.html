The language of our business.

The terms we use, in plain English.

Acquisition
The purchase of a property that meets our underwriting standards.
Value-add
An investment approach that improves a property through renovation or better operations to increase its value.
Scope of work
The defined list of renovation tasks, materials and timeline for a property.
Disposition
The sale of a property, ending our ownership.
Buy and hold
Owning a property long term, typically as a rental, for recurring income and appreciation.
Capital recycling
Reinvesting proceeds from sales into new acquisitions.
Stabilization
The point at which a property is in good condition and leased on an ongoing basis.
Underwriting
The process of analyzing a property and its risks before committing capital.
Asset management
Oversight of a property’s performance and strategy over its life.

Definitions are general and for information only.
