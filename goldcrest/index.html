What we do

Two strategies, one operating platform

We combine active value creation with steady rental income. Renovation and resale generates capital gains; the rental portfolio provides recurring, passive income. Running both lets us keep capital working across market cycles.

Renovate & Sell

We acquire properties that are under-maintained or under-used, complete renovation, and sell them to owner-occupant buyers. Our scale gives us purchasing power with contractors and suppliers and a repeatable process from acquisition to closing.

Learn more

Buy & Hold Rentals

We hold and professionally manage a large portfolio of residential rentals. Diversification across many properties reduces dependence on any single asset and produces consistent rental income.
